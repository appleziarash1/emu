How to play

    

      • Touch anywhere on the left half of the screen: the stick appears under your
        thumb, and the ring follows it, so you never have to hunt for it.

      • STRIKE swings the weapon you are carrying. It also snaps onto a nearby foe,
        so you do not have to aim perfectly.

      • SPELL bursts outward all around you, hurting everything close and knocking
        their shots out of the air. It recharges slowly, so save it for trouble.

      • SPECIAL hurls a blade that cuts through one side of the room and comes
        back to your hand, cutting again on the way home.

      • DASH makes you briefly untouchable — dash through enemies to escape.

      • Clear every shade in a chamber, then pick a god's boon. The arm you chose
        at the start stays with you all the way down.

      • Boons stack. Build a strong run, then beat the Warden.

      • Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, J strike, U spell, I special, Space dash.

      • Controller: left stick to move, X strike, Y special, B spell, A dash,
        Start pause. The touch stick and buttons hide while a controller is
        plugged in, and come back if you unplug it.
