An original Hades-inspired roguelike. Fight your way out of the

How to play

    

      • Left thumb on the round pad moves you.

      • STRIKE swings your blade at whatever is in front.

      • DASH makes you briefly untouchable — dash through enemies to escape.

      • Clear every shade in a chamber, then pick a god's boon.

      • Boons stack. Build a strong run, then beat the Warden.

      • A physical controller or keyboard also works.
