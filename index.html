How to play

    

      • Touch anywhere on the left half of the screen: the stick appears under your
        thumb, and the ring follows it, so you never have to hunt for it.

      • STRIKE swings the weapon you are carrying. It also snaps onto a nearby foe,
        so you do not have to aim perfectly.

      • DASH makes you briefly untouchable — dash through enemies to escape.

      • Clear every shade in a chamber, then pick a god's boon. The arm you chose
        at the start stays with you all the way down.

      • Boons stack. Build a strong run, then beat the Warden.

      • A physical controller or keyboard also works.
